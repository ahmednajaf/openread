Optional detail post

The aim is to make reading less exhausting without another monthly bill.

Voice runs on your device. Optional local models add summaries, questions, dictation and audio overviews. They need a one-time download and use your computer’s resources.

This is an early build. English is strongest; it doesn’t match every Speechify feature, there’s no hosted sync, and installation still takes a few steps.

The prototype began with minutes of vibe coding. Getting playback, highlighting and local models working reliably took further iteration and testing.
