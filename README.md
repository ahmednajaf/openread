# OpenRead

**Reading support shouldn’t need another subscription.**

OpenRead is a free, open-source reading companion for **Safari and Chrome on Mac**. Listen to webpages and documents with natural voices while the words are highlighted as you go.

**[Hear it, watch the demo & install →](https://openread-eosin.vercel.app)**

## What it does

- Reads aloud with local voices, adjustable speed and word highlighting.
- Opens PDFs, scans, EPUBs, Word documents and pasted text.
- Keeps a local library with bookmarks and audio exports.
- Offers optional local AI for summaries, questions, audio overviews and dictation.

No account. No subscription. Your text is processed on your device.

## Get started

1. [Download OpenRead](https://github.com/ahmednajaf/openread/archive/refs/heads/master.zip) and unzip it.
2. Double-click **Set Up OpenRead.command** for the one-time voice setup.
3. Follow the [installation guide](GETTING_STARTED.md) to add it to Safari or Chrome.

This is an early release with manual installation. Setup downloads local models; keep the helper running while you listen. English works best.
