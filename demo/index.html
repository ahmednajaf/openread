A SMALL PAUSE GOES A LONG WAY.01 / ∞

Some days, a page of words feels like an open door. On other days, it feels like a wall. The words haven’t changed. The amount of room we have for them has.

Reading asks us to hold a surprising number of things at once: where we are on the page, what came before, and where the next sentence might take us. It’s a lot of invisible work.

There is more than one way in.

Listening gives a thought another way to reach us. A steady voice can carry the thread while our eyes find their place. We can walk, look away for a moment, or simply let the next sentence arrive.

There’s no perfect speed. Sometimes we need to slow a voice down and sit with an idea. Sometimes a quicker pace makes it easier to stay with the story. The right pace is the one that works for us today.

Start with one small thing.

  • Choose one small thing to read.
    • Start with a single paragraph.
    • Pause whenever you need to.
    Then come back at your own pace.
  • Find a voice that feels comfortable.

One paragraph. One page. A few lines from something we’ve been saving for later. We don’t have to finish everything to make a little progress.

And if we lose our place, we can come back. The words will still be here. A pause is part of the pace.
